## 📜 The Law of the Land

### 1. Don't @ Staff & Respect Dev Boundaries 🚫
Our developers and moderators are here to build and manage the project—not to act as 1:1 help desks.
* **Do not ping (@) developers or staff** unless specifically told to do so.
* **Do not DM staff** for support or special attention. DMs will be ignored, and repeated DMing will get you kicked.
* **No backchanneling.** Asking questions in public channels helps everyone; demand-pings do not.

### 2. Bugs & Ideas Go to GitHub 💡
Got a genius feature idea or found a game-breaking bug? **Do not dump it in general chat.**
* **GitHub is required** for official feature requests and detailed bug reports: https://github.com/noctalia-dev/noctalia
* Keep chat channels for discussion, not issue tracking.

### 3. No Politics or Religion 🚫
The real world is stressful enough. Leave your ballots and bibles at the door. We are here for code, not crusades.

### 4. Don't be a Dick 🛑
Be respectful to the community and the staff. Also, please stop calling everyone "bro." We aren't in a frat house. Read the room. If you have an interpersonal issue with someone, take it off the server.

### 5. Don't Yuck Someone's Yum 🤝
Don't be polarized. If someone likes a language, framework, or topic you hate, just scroll past it. Don't shit on other people's interests just to be edgy.

### 6. Keep it Clean & Composed 🤐
No spamming text channels, no massive strings of random, nonsensical messages, and zero creepy or sexualized behavior. Don't weird other people out.

### 7. The Golden Rule 😇
Be a decent human being. If you wouldn't say it to someone's face, don't type it here.
